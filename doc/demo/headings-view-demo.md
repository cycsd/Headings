# Trip to Kyoto

A short planning note used to record the README screenshots. Every section exercises one thing the view shows.

## Itinerary

### Day 1: Arrival

Land at Kansai, take the Haruka express to Kyoto Station and check in before dinner.

- [x] Book the Haruka ticket
- [ ] Reserve dinner near the station
- [ ] Buy an IC card

### Day 2: Temples

Start early at Fushimi Inari, then Tofuku-ji in the afternoon.

> [!tip] Beat the crowds
> The shrine is open 24 hours; the torii path is almost empty before 7 am.

### Day 3: Arashiyama

Bamboo grove, Tenryu-ji and the monkey park. Keep the evening free.
